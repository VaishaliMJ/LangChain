The sky drapes low in sheets of heavy grey,
As sudden winds sweep all the warmth away.
A distant rumble calls across the plain,
To cue the steady rhythm of the rain.
It breaks the dust with cold and silver beads,
To quench the quiet ache of waiting seeds.
It taps a frantic dance on windowpanes,
And rushes down the cobblestones and drains.
The world slows down beneath the masking blur,
Where memories of older autumns stir.
The air is clean, the heavy spirits rise,
Washed by the torrent of the open skies.
